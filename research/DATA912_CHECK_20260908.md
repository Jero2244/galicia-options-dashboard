# GGAL comparison against Data912

Checked September 8, 2026, approximately 09:21–09:23 Buenos Aires time.
Data912 is useful as a supplementary source, but this check cannot establish
synchronized quote accuracy. The dashboard's data provider was not changed.

## Local GGAL options (ARS)

Data912 returned 464 Argentine option rows, including 87 GFG/GGAL symbols.
The fresh BYMA options panel returned an empty list; the stock panel contained
zero prices. Consequently the comparison uses the saved BYMA snapshot retrieved
September 7 at 13:54 Buenos Aires time. Both local feeds lack full quote dates.
These are differences between snapshots, not a measurement of provider error.

| Check | Result |

| Shared symbols | 83 |

These matches use exact symbols only; Data912's local response does not verify
settlement, expiration or adjusted strike metadata. It contains no IV, Greeks,
open interest or dated trade/quote timestamps. Do not infer ambiguous adjusted
strikes from the symbol or substitute retrieval time for observation time.

Data912-only symbols: GFGC10700D, GFGC11100D, GFGC9900DI, GFGV7600DI.
BYMA-archive-only symbols: GFGV4200SE, GFGV5000SE, GFGV7800DI, GFGV8000DI.
This may reflect changes in the panel between retrievals, rather than missing
coverage at the same instant.

## US GGAL ADR (USD)

The [US option-chain endpoint](https://data912.apidocs.ar/operations/geteodoptionchain)
is explicitly EOD. `/eod/option_chain/GGAL` returned HTTP 500 twice. A lowercase
GGAL request and an AAPL control also returned HTTP 500. No Data912 option chain
was available to compare contract prices, IV or Greeks with the saved Alpaca
indicative chain. The evidence suggests an endpoint problem at retrieval time;
it does not establish a permanent lack of GGAL coverage.

Data912's ADR panel returned GGAL price 44.39, no bid/ask, zero volume and no
market timestamp. Read-only Alpaca historical requests returned September 4 bars:

| Price source | USD | Data912 minus reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data912 reported ADR price (undated) | 44.39 | — |
| Alpaca SIP daily-bar close, September 4 | 44.36 | +0.03 (+0.068%) |
| Alpaca IEX daily-bar close, September 4 | 44.32 | +0.07 (+0.158%) |

The prices are close but not identical. Without Data912's observation date and
closing-price convention, this does not establish same-session agreement.
A daily-bar close is not necessarily an official closing-auction price.
Historical SIP stock bars responding successfully does not establish OPRA
option-quote entitlements.

The [US volatility summary](https://data912.apidocs.ar/operations/geteodvolatilities)
responded with short/medium/long IV of 0.4623 / 0.4767 / 0.47462 and HV of
0.386938 / 0.494672 / 0.63278. Interpreted as decimal volatilities, these are
46.23% / 47.67% / 47.462% IV and 38.6938% / 49.4672% / 63.278% HV.
The payload supplies neither an as-of date nor tenor definitions or calculation
details. Its short IV being near the project's 46.12 ADR research index does
not validate that index: the metrics and constituent methodology are unverified.

Run `python research/check_data912.py --compare` to reproduce the local comparison

to the September 7 BYMA archive; it is not a general synchronized-feed validator.

The observed Data912 live responses advertised `Cache-Control: public, max-age=30`;
the volatility summary advertised 3600 seconds. HTTP caching and response dates
do not prove market-data freshness. The [documentation overview](https://data912.apidocs.ar/)
also warns that the data is not real time. No refresh-speed guarantee is inferred.

Validation: captured and parsed the actual responses; executed the offline
comparison and checked representative matched rows. No dashboard code changed.
